\begin{abstract}
Small and medium-sized businesses that buy metals pay prices set on commodity
exchanges, and they rarely have the tools to judge whether a supplier's quote
is fair or whether to buy now or wait. ProcurePulse addresses this problem. We
collected daily data from January 2010 to October 2026 for four COMEX metals
(gold, silver, copper and platinum) and four economic signals (the US dollar
index, crude oil, the 10-year Treasury yield and inflation), cleaned it and
built 27 features. We then predicted whether the gold price will be higher in
five trading days, comparing a majority baseline, logistic regression, a
random forest, histogram gradient boosting and an LSTM network. The models
were tuned with walk-forward validation on 2010--2021, selected on 2022--2023
and tested once on 701 trading days from 2024 to October 2026. The signal is
weak: the best test AUC-ROC is 0.60, logistic regression gives the most
reliable ``up'' calls (precision 0.70) and the LSTM gives the most balanced
predictions (F1 0.55), while the tree models predict ``down'' on every test
day because of a shift in price levels. We turned these results into
Buy, Hold and Negotiate recommendations: each quote is compared with an
expected five-day price range built from the 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles
of past five-day price changes, and for gold the model's predicted direction
decides quotes inside that range. Finally, we built a local Streamlit
application that runs this inference in real time on built-in, uploaded or
freshly downloaded prices and explains every recommendation.
\end{abstract}
